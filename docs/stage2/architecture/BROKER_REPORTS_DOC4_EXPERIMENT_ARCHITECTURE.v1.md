# Broker Reports DOC4 Experiment Architecture v1

Status: `INACTIVE_OFFLINE_EXPERIMENT_ONLY`

DOC4 introduces no product or canonical financial authority. Its only consumer is `scripts/run_pdf_view_semantic_experiment.py`; no package export, OpenWebUI action, product prompt, valve, admission path, fallback, or live route imports it.

| Operation | Sole owner | Output | Forbidden consumers |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| paired native-PDF/complete-View execution and exact preflight | `PdfViewSemanticExperimentRunner` | ignored private experiment evidence | product, Gate 2 qualification, activation |
| outbound authorization | `OpenAiDoc4Transport` exact request-hash allowlist | only run-plan-bound OpenAI requests | symbolic-ID-only authorization, arbitrary probes, extra synthetic provider calls |
| response validation and source-grounded pointers | `validate_semantic_response` | validated semantic response | coordinate-only or excerpt-only claims |
| source-blind cross-arm comparison | `PdfViewSemanticComparator.compare` | hash-bound comparison | PDF, View, gold, provider, LLM |
| independent gold and source adjudication sealing | `PdfViewSemanticAdjudicationFactory` | hash-bound gold/adjudication | either experiment arm |
| terminal four-document result | `PdfViewSemanticResultFactory.finalize` | one hash-bound aggregate after exactly 4 eligible, 4 paired, 4 adjudicated | single-document `PASSED_*`, partial corpus |

The active Gate 2 provider adapters cannot be reused: their contract projects text-only product semantic requests and provider responses, while DOC4 must give one arm a native PDF and the other the complete DOC3 View with separate stateless HTTP sessions. Extending the product adapter would broaden product reachability and violate arm isolation. DOC4 therefore owns one bounded, non-reusable provider seam.

The historical global authority map remains byte-exact because DOC4 changes none of its product domains and that file is hash-bound by earlier architecture audit evidence. DOC4 ownership is declared here and in the five normative DOC4 schemas. DOC1 schema, DOC2 builder, DOC3 renderer, generated bundles, and all product routes remain unchanged.

Before any provider call, the private operator receipt is bound to the exact four PDF and View SHA-256 values, final run-plan SHA-256, and the aggregate SHA-256 of every permitted `endpoint + request-body` pair. The transport rejects any pair outside that allowlist and rechecks the frozen model, `store=false`, empty tools, reasoning and sampling policy immediately before sending. DOC2 coverage schema/integrity/acceptance and DOC3 receipt schema/full-coverage are validated, and the complete View plus receipt are regenerated byte-for-byte from the validated Managed Document. The synthetic injection fixture remains offline-only and never reaches the provider under the four-document authorization.

Standalone response validation is source-bound through the exact corpus manifest and safe ID. Per-document adjudication emits only a non-terminal `DOCUMENT_*` assessment; the `finalize` mode is the sole path that can emit terminal semantic equivalence. Its factory independently replays the integrity-bound preflight/run-plan relation plus exact gold and paired-response hashes for every adjudication, and fails closed on rebadging or unless the exact four documents are eligible, paired, source-grounded, response-bound, gold-bound, and integrity-valid.
